My Pocket DJ is an AI-first music technology platform currently in development. It is designed to help everyday music lovers create professional-sounding DJ mixes through proactive musical intelligence.

“Given what you're playing now, here are the three best ways you can go next.”
The Idea

The AI doesn't just give you DJ tools. It understands the music.

Traditional DJ software provides decks, waveforms, BPM, effects and cue points—but still expects the user to know what to play next, when to transition and how to make that transition work musically.

My Pocket DJ is being developed around a different approach: put the complexity inside the intelligence layer and give the user simple, musically meaningful choices.

Musical Intelligence Layer

Understand what's happening inside the music.

Rather than treating a track as a fixed BPM, key and genre, My Pocket DJ is designed to build a time-aligned understanding of how the music evolves.

01

Structure

Understand musical sections and how a track changes through time.

02

Rhythm & Tempo

03

Harmony

Reason about harmonic relationships between musical moments—not simply track labels.

04

Energy & Mood

Follow changes in intensity, dynamics and emotional character throughout the music.

Proactive AI

Three ways forward.

Instead of making the user search through hundreds of tracks, the AI is designed to recommend a small number of musically meaningful directions based on what is happening right now.

Keep the vibe

Maintain the current musical character, mood and energy.

Build the energy

Move naturally toward something stronger and more intense.

Change direction

Deliberately shift the mood, style or musical character.

Transition Intelligence

Not only what comes next. When and how.

Music recommendation is only part of the problem. DJing also requires understanding when a transition should begin, where the next track should enter and whether the musical sections will work together.

What should come next?

Evaluate possible tracks against the evolving musical context.

When should we transition?

Identify musically meaningful transition windows rather than relying on a single fixed point.

How should they connect?

Reason about structure, energy, rhythm and harmony to guide the transition.

Simple for the user.
Sophisticated underneath.

Who It's For

For people who love music—not only professional DJs.

My Pocket DJ is being designed for music lovers who know what sounds good but may not understand professional DJ terminology, harmonic mixing, phrasing or complex DJ software.

The goal is professional-quality musical decision support through an interface that remains approachable for beginners.
